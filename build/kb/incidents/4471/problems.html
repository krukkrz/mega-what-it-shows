  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and these tenants write the day first. 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, but it reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them, and the form swaps it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs a hint in the tenant’s order.
  • shift: the date is right and the day moves by one. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May, a meeting reaches Lisbon on Tuesday and Toronto on Monday, an invite arrives before its booking. That is a time-zone conversion, not a swap of day and month, so neither fix above ends it.

How the count was made

  • One report per id: the export repeats some reports, and uniqueById drops the copies.
  • The cause comes from the tenant, not from intake. Intake tags from the reporter’s browser, so its hint and parser tags disagree with the cause in both directions (the “intake tagged as” column).
  • The shift reports are the ones the desk itself read as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The #4471 page counted them under parser and footnoted them. Here they are a problem of their own. The logic is in src/lib/kb/problems.ts.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. Every report here has a subject about a wrong date, so three bugs with the same symptom became one ticket. The decision accepted that cost for different subjects about one bug. It did not plan for one subject over different bugs.
